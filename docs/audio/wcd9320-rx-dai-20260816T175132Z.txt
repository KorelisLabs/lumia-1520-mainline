registers moved in 0x030-0x1b0, and after teardown none did.

so none of this went near the codec's register cache -- which
still reports 460 cacheable registers agreeing with the chip.

WHAT THIS RUN CLAIMS. The port path was reached through the
research hook -- origin=manual -- not through ASoC.

callback -- that is the minimal-card milestone's claim and
is evidenced separately. What it shows is that the manual
entry point still produces the same hardware transition
with a card present.

No SLIMbus channel was allocated, defined or activated:
configuring the codec's side of a port is not a data path.
Nothing has streamed and nothing has made a sound.
