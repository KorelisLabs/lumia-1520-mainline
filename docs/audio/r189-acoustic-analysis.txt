Verdict: Secondary MI2S, on either data line, with the 5 V rail up, does not
sound the loudspeaker at -40 dBFS. The DSP accepts the port and the bit clock.

What re-reading Windows' tables then showed (r190 mapping):
  AUDD.PPNF maps Windows' audio endpoint ids to power components. Endpoints 0x45
  and 0x43 switch components 0 (the codec) AND 1 (SMPS4, 5 V / 1.5 A) -- the 5 V
  boost goes with the CODEC, not with the I2S pins (components 4 and 5 belong to
  endpoints 0x46/0x51 and 0x4A). Downstream's WCD9320 driver has a VDD_SPKDRV
  supply (regulator "cdc-vdd-spkdrv") on SPK DAC -- the class-D speaker driver's
  own supply. r187 drove SPK_OUT live with that supply never enabled.
