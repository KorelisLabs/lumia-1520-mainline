=== finding ===
THE QDSP6 CONSUMES PERIODS AT REAL TIME.

One ASM RUN was issued and acknowledged -- the first WRITE followed
it, which is the acknowledgement made visible. 154 period
completions then arrived over 3.0156s, a sustained
48707.1 frames/s against a target of 48000.

hw_ptr advanced by 146880 frames, matching the completion count
times the period size. On this platform that is not a coincidence:
pcm_irq_pos is incremented only in the WRITE_DONE handler, so the
pointer IS the DSP's acknowledgement count.

The WCD9320's SLIMbus RX stream was enabled before the first period
and torn down after the last, and ASoC drove all of it -- the
research hook was not involved.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLAIM. The driver writes to the DSP whether or
not userspace supplied data, and there is no receiver-side counter
on this hardware, so this does not prove the bytes written here
reached the codec. Until the codec-absent negative control runs,
'the DSP consumed' and 'SLIMbus carried it' are concurrent facts,
not a proven chain. Nothing was audible: there is no output path.
